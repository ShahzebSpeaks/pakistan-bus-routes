# Code of Conduct

This project is for everyone who uses public transport in Pakistan, whatever their city, language, gender, religion, ethnicity, ability or experience with technology.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Assume good faith. Data mistakes are normal. Fix them kindly.
- Keep discussion about transit data and the software.
- English, Urdu and Roman Urdu are all welcome in issues and pull requests.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Political, sectarian or ethnic attacks.
- Posting other people's personal information.
- Spam, advertising or deliberately wrong data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository profile.
