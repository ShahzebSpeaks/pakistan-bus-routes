BOTTOM LINE
No source anywhere publishes a per-route timetable (first/last bus per terminal, headway, run time) for the
Peoples Bus Service. SMTA's route pages (smta.gos.pk/routes/N) list only distance, bus length ("12 M / 9 M" =
bus length in metres, NOT minutes) and stops. Almost every news/blog article repeats one network-wide claim.
Journey time: no source gives one for any route (journey_min is null everywhere). No Friday/Sunday difference
was found in any source; redbus.shop (Jul 2026) says service runs daily incl. weekends and holidays.

NOTES FOR MERGING
- "official": true is set only for SMTA notices and Sindh-minister / SMTA-MD statements quoted in news.
- "ALL-RED"/"ALL-EV" rows are network-wide claims, not route ids; apply to system hours or every route of that type.
- EV5 and DD1 rows repeat round4 entries (re-verified); the pink Bol Apr-2024 row repeats round4's Bol figure.
