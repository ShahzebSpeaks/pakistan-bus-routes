## Per-source notes

**1. PTC LOV fare tables (Lahore).** This is the most valuable find. Each route has a scanned one-page Urdu fare table with ordered stops, the distance between stops, cumulative km and a full fare matrix. Each route also has an alignment image (`/system/files/LOV%20101.jpeg`). I transcribed LOV-101 (29 stops, 34.4 km, Rs 14–34). The other 14 are images and need manual or OCR transcription. The page title calls these "Urban (LOV) Wagon / Mini Bus"; PTC's HOV page has only EV route 03. The fares follow a 2020 notification and are probably stale. Whether each route still runs is unverified.

**2. RTA Karachi fare list, 4 April 2026.** Official, and it confirms that private buses, minibuses and coaches still operate. It says fares "shall remain as it was prior to 27 Feb 2026".
- Bus: Rs 40 up to 10 km, Rs 70 up to 30 km, Rs 110 above 31 km.
- Minibus: Rs 50, Rs 80, Rs 120 for the same bands.
- Coach: same as minibus.

Use these as the system `fare_rules` for any Karachi private-route system.

**3. PTA Sindh intercity fares, 4 April 2026.** 20 routes (Karachi to Hyderabad 160 km … Karachi to Badin 211 km). Per-km rates: deluxe non-AC bus Rs 3.30, non-AC van Rs 3.10, AC bus Rs 3.80, AC van Rs 3.50. These are intercity routes, so they are out of scope unless the dataset adds intercity.

**4. URC Bus Routes Karachi 2010.** The only complete Karachi private-route inventory found. The PDF text extracts cleanly. Its last line credits a 2009 blog, so it is an NGO republication rather than an official list. There are some duplicate IDs (16-F, 17-I, 17-J, 18-K, 5-A) and about 90 named coach services. A large share of these routes have died since 2010, so import everything with `status: "unverified"` and confirm through 2025-26 news or fieldwork.

**5/6. PMA IPTN Lahore and Multan.** These are planning tables. Lahore Phase-I (routes 1-14) roughly matches today's Speedo routes, but the numbering differs from the current RT-xx numbers in `research/lahore.json`. They are useful for alignments, lengths and headways, and routes 15-122 can be marked `planned`. The Multan PDF's text layer misaligns the route numbers (Phase-I shows 11 route numbers but only 10 alignments), so render it to an image before transcribing.

**7. CDA transit map.** I built the route codes and descriptions from text coordinates, so the pairing is reliable. Most stop names are scattered map labels. Only FR-12 (18 numbered stops) and Route 14A (14 numbered stops) have clean lists. FR-12's list does not reach Taxila, so it may show only a segment.

**8. TransPeshawar data book.** An official list of stops and launch dates for 7 feeder routes. The table rows may be sorted by stop ID or by entry, not by route order: DR-3A lists Dabgari before Kohat Adda even though its stop IDs run from 125 upward. Use it to check the existing Peshawar data, not to replace it.

**9. Yellow Line EIA.** Station names are authoritative for the planned corridor. The direct and feeder routes give termini, km and stop counts only.

## What to extract next

1. **Transcribe the 14 remaining PTC LOV fare tables.** Render with PyMuPDF at 110 dpi and read the Urdu, which gives about 350 stops for Lahore private routes. Also check `ptc.punjab.gov.pk/route_permit` and look for similar PTC or RTA pages for Faisalabad, Rawalpindi and Gujranwala wagon routes.
2. **Import URC 2010 as `karachi-private-2010`**, with all 326 routes marked `unverified` and the fares from source 2. Then check which routes are still alive against 2024-26 news (Dawn/Tribune stories on "route W-11", "4K", "X-5" and so on).
3. **Lahore IPTN routes 15-122 and Multan IPTN** as planned routes (via-points go in notes, not stops).
4. **Reorder the Yellow Line stations** using the OSM corridor; cross-check the Peshawar feeder stop order against OSM route relations.
5. **Ask directly for:** the RTA Karachi route-permit register (RTI request under the Sindh Transparency & RTI Act 2016), the SMTA route directory PDF, and the Quetta Green Bus timetable. None of these are published online.

## What is impossible to get online

- An official, current list of Karachi private routes and their stops. RTA/DRTA publish fares and permit counts but no route register.
- Timetables for private routes; none exist. Headways appear only in the PMA planning tables and the Zu 2022 book.
- The Karachi Master Plan 2047 transport annexes, a data.gov.pk transit dataset, or GTFS files from any Pakistani operator.
