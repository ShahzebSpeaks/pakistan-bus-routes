Privacy Policy

Pakistan Bus Routes (website and Android app) · Last updated 1 November 2026

Pakistan Bus Routes is a free, open-source route guide and trip planner for public transport in Pakistan. The Android app shows the same website, shahzebspeaks.github.io/pakistan-bus-routes, full screen.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Your location

If you tap "Nearby" or "use my location", your browser or phone asks for permission. Your location is used only on your device to find stops near you and to plan a trip. It is never sent to us or stored by us. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in your phone or browser settings.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the route data and your settings (city, language, light or dark theme) on your device so it works offline. This stays on your device. Clearing the app's storage removes it.

Services the app connects to

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web server it may log technical data such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles are loaded from tile.openstreetmap.org, which sees your IP address. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Reporting a mistake opens a public GitHub issue form. Anything you write there is public and is handled under GitHub's terms. Using it is optional.

Children

The app is a general-audience route guide. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ShahzebSpeaks/pakistan-bus-routes/issues.
